# Product Vision

## One-sentence definition

`space.k-y.cc` is a personally owned digital space for short and long personal writing, paired with a private AI-curated interest reader that turns twice-daily news collection into calm, source-backed Editions.

## Why it exists

The owner already has accounts on large social platforms, but publishing across many public services is fragmented, time-consuming, and unsuitable for private or semi-private writing. This site provides:

- ownership of the primary publishing surface;
- one coherent identity across personal projects;
- control over visibility and retention;
- a private place for material intended only for the owner;
- an interest reader that reduces news overload;
- an archive that remains searchable by text, topic, tag, and date.

## Recommended model

Do not build a miniature public social network. Build a personal publication with an owner workspace.

### Reader-facing content

- **Note** — short personal thought, update, reaction, or small story.
- **Article** — longer personal writing, review, travel record, project reflection, or essay.
- **Edition** — a finished AI-assisted digest for one topic and date range.

### Owner-only editorial content

- **Source Item** — one external feed/API item.
- **Story Cluster** — one real-world event represented by multiple Source Items.
- **Edition Draft** — selected Story Clusters assembled for review.

This model solves the largest information-design risk: automated news volume does not flood the personal stream.

## Core principles

1. **Owned first** — the canonical copy lives on `space.k-y.cc`.
2. **Private by design** — visibility is explicit; private content is never committed to GitHub.
3. **Calm over infinite** — finite Editions are preferred over endless raw news feeds.
4. **Human authorship remains visible** — the owner’s Notes and Articles look distinct from AI-assisted Editions.
5. **Source traceability** — every external claim can be traced to original URLs and timestamps.
6. **Small-system discipline** — one repository and one main Worker until evidence requires separation.
7. **Searchable memory** — archive, search, tags, and dates are product foundations, not later add-ons.
8. **Original visual identity** — all public design language and assets are generic and original.

## Success criteria

The project is successful when the owner can:

- publish a short Note in under one minute;
- draft and publish a long Article without touching Git;
- find an old post by phrase, tag, category, channel, month, or date range;
- review twice-daily collected news in a private Inbox;
- receive one useful daily Edition per active topic without duplicate stories;
- keep selected content private while publishing other content publicly;
- link the project naturally from `k-y.cc` alongside other projects.

## Explicit non-goals

- building a public community;
- maximizing page views or advertising revenue;
- mirroring full external articles;
- producing high-volume SEO content;
- imitating a specific existing entertainment property;
- storing private content inside the public repository.
